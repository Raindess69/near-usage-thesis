# THESIS: NEAR's value now accrues through NEAR Intents, and it covers 19.0% of issuance

## Claim

The long-term case for holding NEAR depends on whether usage removes NEAR from circulation as fast as the protocol mints it.
On today's evidence:

1. **Issuance is about 2.49% a year and gas burn barely dents it.** Measured directly from block headers over the
   last 120 epochs (2026-09-28 to 2026-11-02), the protocol minted 3.19M NEAR and burnt 36.7K NEAR: burn offset
   1.15% of issuance. L1 transaction counts, however large, do not move this number.
2. **NEAR Intents is the only material sink.** In the last 90 days (2026-08-04 to 2026-11-01) NEAR Intents revenue was
   $4.75M (1.44M NEAR at daily prices), 13x the chain's gas fees, and offset 18.0% of issuance.
   Together with burn, usage paid for **19.0%** of the 8.02M NEAR minted.
3. **Break-even is far but measurable.** At the last 90 days' capture rate (4.1 bps of volume) and price, usage-based
   capture has to grow **5.3x**. If that growth comes from Intents, it means about $715.07M of Intents volume per day
   versus $127.59M now (5.6x). At the year-over-year volume growth seen so far (342%, a launch-phase rate)
   that takes 1.1 years; at 50% a year it takes 4.1 years, with price and capture rate held where they are.

So the investable thesis is narrow: **NEAR is a bet that NEAR Intents volume grows roughly 5.6x at a stable or rising capture
rate, and that governance turns the bought-back NEAR into a real supply reduction.** It is not a bet on L1 activity: L1
transactions are down 91% from their peak month (2024-12) while Intents volume kept growing.

## Evidence for

- **Issuance really was halved, and the measurement is solid.** Gross issuance ran at 4.98% a year before 2025-10-30
  and 2.49% after it. 107 archival block headers sampled since genesis match the indexer's daily supply within
  0.017% (median 0.0068%), and indexer-based minting agrees with direct RPC minting within 0.29%.
- **NEAR Intents generates real revenue that L1 gas never did.** Over 90 days Intents revenue was 13x the chain's gas
  fees ($4.75M vs $352.1K) and supplied 95% of all usage-based value capture.
- **NEAR keeps a growing share of each Intents dollar.** Captured revenue rose from 2.1 bps of volume in 2025-11 to
  5.5 bps in 2026-10.
- **Revenue in USD is growing.** Average monthly Intents revenue in 2026-08..2026-10 was 3.0x its Q1 2026 level in USD.
- **The official revenue dashboard is broadly reproducible from independent public data.** Over 10 complete months of 2026 our
  estimate differs from revenue.near.org by 3.9 pp on average (7 of 10 months within 5 pp; our mean
  15.4% vs official 15.8%).
- **Bought-back NEAR is visible onchain.** `buybacks.multisignature.near` held 2.16M NEAR on 2026-11-02 (first sample above
  10K NEAR: 2026-04-27), and `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, another revenue wallet listed on revenue.near.org, held
  1.97M NEAR (above 10K NEAR since 2025-11-08). DefiLlama attributes 3.16M NEAR of buybacks to the period since 2026-02-23;
  the buyback wallet alone holds 68% of that amount.

## Evidence against

- **Usage still pays for only a fraction of issuance.** 19.0% over 90 days, 15.3% year to date, and between 8.0%
  (2026-04) and 26.6% (2026-02) per month in 2026. Effective emissions since the buyback program started were
  still 18.92M NEAR of 22.32M NEAR minted.
- **A rising price raises the bar.** Issuance is fixed in NEAR while revenue is earned in USD. Average monthly Intents revenue in
  2026-08..2026-10 was 3.0x its Q1 2026 level in USD but only 1.2x in NEAR, because the price rose 2.5x. Any rally that is
  not matched by revenue lowers the share of issuance that usage covers.
- **L1 activity collapsed.** Daily L1 transactions changed -79%, active accounts -64% and gas used -59%
  between Q1 2026 and the last 90 days. The step change in April 2026 (-74% transactions month over month) shows up
  in two independent indexers (fees -69% in NearBlocks, -69% in DefiLlama/Allium), so it is not an
  indexing artifact. Its cause cannot be identified from aggregate data.
- **Buybacks are held, not burned.** The bought-back NEAR sits as native balances in a multisig and a DAO account. NEAR's own tokenomics post says
  governance "would retain control of the revenue, with the ability to redeploy assets" and that "no specific mechanism is guaranteed".
  Total supply keeps growing at 2.49% a year regardless.
- **No usage series reliably leads price.** 0 of 10 weekly lead-lag tests pass a Holm-adjusted 5% threshold
  (0 before correction). The history is short (74 weeks), so this is weak evidence either way.

## Break-even scenarios (not forecasts)

Years until usage-based capture covers issuance if Intents revenue grows at a constant rate, with NEAR price and capture rate
held at their last-90-day values. Required growth: 5.3x.

| Revenue growth per year | Years to break-even |
| --- | --- |
| 25% | 7.5 |
| 50% | 4.1 |
| 100% | 2.4 |
| 200% | 1.5 |
| 342% (observed YoY volume growth) | 1.1 |

## What would change our mind

- **Toward bullish:** usage covering at least 50% of issuance over two consecutive 90-day windows; net capture above
  4.1 bps while Intents volume keeps growing year over year; an onchain burn or lock of the NEAR held by
  `buybacks.multisignature.near`; or a lead-lag test from usage to price that survives correction after at least another year of data.
- **Toward bearish:** 90-day coverage below 10% (it was 19.0%); year-over-year Intents volume growth turning negative
  (it was 4.4x); the buyback wallet balance falling (largest drop between samples so far: 0 NEAR); or governance
  redirecting captured revenue away from buybacks.
- **On the method:** if an independent source (BigQuery, the Intents Explorer API) disagrees with DefiLlama's Intents revenue
  by more than the gap we see against revenue.near.org (10.2 pp in 2026-02), the coverage numbers here are wrong.

## What we could not compute

- **NEAR Intents Explorer API.** It needs a partner JWT. We use DefiLlama's open-source `near-intents` adapter instead, which
  states that it reconciles with revenue.near.org; chart 4 checks that claim.
- **BigQuery (`bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us`).** Queries need a billed Google Cloud project. We use the NearBlocks
  indexer and check it against archival RPC block headers and against DefiLlama/Allium.
- **Staking and locking.** NEAR's "effective emissions" definition also subtracts staked or locked NEAR; our effective emissions
  subtract only burn and buybacks.
- **Revenue by channel** (front-end, partners, quote improvement) is shown on revenue.near.org but not in a public time series.
- **NEAR AI / Agent Market revenue** has no public time series.
- **The cause of the April 2026 L1 activity drop** cannot be identified from aggregate counts.
