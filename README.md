# NEAR Usage Thesis: can NEAR usage pay for NEAR issuance?

An evidence-first investment thesis for NEAR, built for the **NEAR HACKATHON, Investment Thesis track (`thesis`)**.
It answers one question with reproducible public data: **how much of NEAR's token issuance is offset by usage
(gas burn + NEAR Intents revenue used for buybacks), and what would it take to reach break-even?**

- **Thesis:** [THESIS.md](THESIS.md) has the claim, evidence for, evidence against, what would change our mind, and what we could not compute.
- **Dashboard:** [docs/index.html](docs/index.html) has 12 charts. Each one shows the data source, the method, the code that made it, and one plain sentence. Hosted copy (GitHub Pages): https://raindess69.github.io/near-usage-thesis/
- **One command rebuilds everything from raw data:** `make build` (offline, from the committed snapshot) or `make all` (refetch + rebuild).

**Result (data as of 2026-11-01, generated by `make build`):**

- NEAR mints **2.49%** of supply per year (89.3K NEAR per day), measured from block headers over 120 epochs.
- Gas burn offsets **1.15%** of that. L1 transaction counts do not matter for supply.
- NEAR Intents revenue, used for buybacks since 2026-02-23, offset **18.0%** over the last 90 days: 13x the chain's gas fees.
- Together, usage paid for **19.0%** of issuance over 90 days and **15.3%** year to date (official dashboard: 15.8%).
- To cover issuance at today's price, usage-based revenue must grow **5.3x** (about $715.07M of Intents volume per day, now $127.59M).
- Lead-lag: **0 of 10** weekly tests (usage vs price, Intents vs L1 usage) are significant after Holm correction.

## Try it (each step: do X, see Y)

Requirements: Python 3.9+ and `make` (or run the `python -m nut ...` commands directly). No API keys, no accounts, no wallet.

1. **Install.** `python3 -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate && pip install -r requirements.txt`
   You get four libraries: numpy, pandas, matplotlib, requests.
2. **Rebuild from the committed raw snapshot (offline, about 10 seconds).** `make build`
   The last line reads `build: as-of <date>; 12 charts`. The command rewrites `data/processed/*.csv`, `docs/charts/*.svg|png`,
   `docs/metrics.json`, `docs/index.html` and `THESIS.md`.
3. **Check reproducibility.** `make verify`
   It rebuilds into a temporary directory and prints `verify: docs/metrics.json matches the rebuild`, plus the same check for
   `docs/index.html` and `THESIS.md`.
4. **Run the tests (offline).** `make test`
   You see 9 tests pass: the lead-lag statistics on synthetic data, measured issuance against the protocol parameter, the indexer's
   supply against RPC block headers, no unfilled placeholders in THESIS.md, and a source and sentence for every chart.
5. **Open the dashboard.** Open `docs/index.html` in a browser. It shows 12 charts. The "Live check" box
   recomputes the 30-day coverage ratio in your browser from DefiLlama and NearBlocks right now (issuance valued at today's
   price), so you can compare it with the snapshot.
6. **Refresh with today's data.** `make fetch-http && make build` refreshes the HTTP sources (about 10 seconds) and keeps the RPC
   snapshot. `make all` refetches everything, including about 400 archival RPC calls. Public RPCs are rate limited, so that takes
   10-30 minutes. Responses are cached in `.cache/`, so an interrupted fetch resumes. Every number in THESIS.md and on the
   dashboard updates, because none of them are typed by hand.

## How NEAR fits

The project measures NEAR itself. Issuance and burn are read straight from NEAR block headers through archival JSON-RPC:
`total_supply` at every epoch boundary, `chunks[].balance_burnt`, and `next_epoch_id`, which links each epoch to the previous one.
The protocol's own parameters come from `EXPERIMENTAL_protocol_config`. Buyback holdings are read with `view_account`.
NEAR Intents, NEAR's cross-chain settlement layer, supplies the revenue side.
Indexers (NearBlocks, DefiLlama/Allium) are used for daily history and are checked against the chain and against each other.

## Method in brief

- **Minted per day** = change in total supply + burn, where burn = beta x reported transaction fees. beta is measured, not
  assumed: archival RPC burn divided by NearBlocks fees over fully covered days.
- **Coverage** = (burn + NEAR Intents revenue converted to NEAR at the daily price) / minted, over 30 and 90 days, year to date,
  since the buyback program started (2026-02-23), and per month.
- **Break-even multiple** = USD value of issuance / USD value of capture over 90 days. Scenario years = ln(multiple) / ln(1 + growth).
- **Lead-lag** = Granger-style F tests on weekly log changes, with p-values from circular shifts and a Holm correction. Cross-correlations
  are tested the same way.
- **Data checks**: RPC headers vs indexer supply, DefiLlama vs NearBlocks fees, NearBlocks vs CoinGecko price, and our coverage vs
  the official dashboard.

## Limitations

The NEAR Intents revenue history is short: the fee switch dates from late 2025 and buybacks from February 2026. DefiLlama's adapter
is a third-party reconstruction. Coverage depends on NEAR's price. Break-even rows are scenarios, not predictions. Staking and
locking are not modeled. See "What we could not compute" in THESIS.md.
