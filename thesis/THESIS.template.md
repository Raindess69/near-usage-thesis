## Claim

The long-term case for holding NEAR depends on whether usage removes NEAR from circulation as fast as the protocol mints it.
On today's evidence:

1. **Issuance is about {{rpc_issuance}} a year and gas burn barely dents it.** Measured directly from block headers over the
   last {{rpc_epochs}} epochs ({{rpc_from}} to {{rpc_to}}), the protocol minted {{rpc_minted}} and burnt {{rpc_burnt}}: burn offset
   {{rpc_burn_over_mint}} of issuance. L1 transaction counts, however large, do not move this number.
2. **NEAR Intents is the only material sink.** In the last 90 days ({{d90_start}} to {{d90_end}}) NEAR Intents revenue was
   {{d90_rev_usd}} ({{d90_rev_near}} at daily prices), {{d90_rev_over_gas}} the chain's gas fees, and offset {{d90_cov_int}} of issuance.
   Together with burn, usage paid for **{{d90_cov}}** of the {{d90_minted}} minted.
3. **Break-even is far but measurable.** At the last 90 days' capture rate ({{be_bps}} bps of volume) and price, usage-based
   capture has to grow **{{be_multiple}}**. If that growth comes from Intents, it means about {{be_req_day}} of Intents volume per day
   versus {{be_cur_day}} now ({{be_vol_mult}}). At the year-over-year volume growth seen so far ({{be_obs_g}}, a launch-phase rate)
   that takes {{be_obs_years}} years; at 50% a year it takes {{be_50_years}} years, with price and capture rate held where they are.

So the investable thesis is narrow: **NEAR is a bet that NEAR Intents volume grows roughly {{be_vol_mult}} at a stable or rising capture
rate, and that governance turns the bought-back NEAR into a real supply reduction.** It is not a bet on L1 activity: L1
transactions are down {{txns_peak_drop}} from their peak month ({{txns_peak_m}}) while Intents volume kept growing.

## Evidence for

- **Issuance really was halved, and the measurement is solid.** Gross issuance ran at {{rate_before}} a year before {{halving_date}}
  and {{rate_after}} after it. {{supply_samples}} archival block headers sampled since genesis match the indexer's daily supply within
  {{supply_max_diff}} (median {{supply_median_diff}}), and indexer-based minting agrees with direct RPC minting within {{mint_check_abs}}.
- **NEAR Intents generates real revenue that L1 gas never did.** Over 90 days Intents revenue was {{d90_rev_over_gas}} the chain's gas
  fees ({{d90_rev_usd}} vs {{d90_gas_usd}}) and supplied {{d90_share_int}} of all usage-based value capture.
- **NEAR keeps a growing share of each Intents dollar.** Captured revenue rose from {{bps_first}} bps of volume in {{bps_first_m}} to
  {{bps_last}} bps in {{last_month}}.
- **Revenue in USD is growing.** Average monthly Intents revenue in {{growth_to}} was {{rev_usd_mult}} its {{growth_from}} level in USD.
- **The official revenue dashboard is broadly reproducible from independent public data.** Over {{off_n}} complete months of 2026 our
  estimate differs from revenue.near.org by {{off_mad}} pp on average ({{off_within5}} of {{off_n}} months within 5 pp; our mean
  {{ours_mean}} vs official {{off_mean}}).
- **Bought-back NEAR is visible onchain.** `buybacks.multisignature.near` held {{bb_latest}} on {{bb_date}} (first sample above
  10K NEAR: {{bb_first10k}}), and `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, another revenue wallet listed on revenue.near.org, held
  {{fe_latest}} (above 10K NEAR since {{fe_first10k}}). DefiLlama attributes {{bb_dl}} of buybacks to the period since {{sb_start}};
  the buyback wallet alone holds {{bb_ratio_pct}} of that amount.

## Evidence against

- **Usage still pays for only a fraction of issuance.** {{d90_cov}} over 90 days, {{ytd_cov}} year to date, and between {{cov26_min}}
  ({{cov26_min_m}}) and {{cov26_max}} ({{cov26_max_m}}) per month in 2026. Effective emissions since the buyback program started were
  still {{sb_eff}} of {{sb_minted}} minted.
- **A rising price raises the bar.** Issuance is fixed in NEAR while revenue is earned in USD. Average monthly Intents revenue in
  {{growth_to}} was {{rev_usd_mult}} its {{growth_from}} level in USD but only {{rev_near_mult}} in NEAR, because the price rose {{price_mult}}. Any rally that is
  not matched by revenue lowers the share of issuance that usage covers.
- **L1 activity collapsed.** Daily L1 transactions changed {{txns_q1_chg}}, active accounts {{aa_q1_chg}} and gas used {{gas_q1_chg}}
  between Q1 2026 and the last 90 days. The step change in April 2026 ({{txns_mar_apr}} transactions month over month) shows up
  in two independent indexers (fees {{fees_mar_apr_nb}} in NearBlocks, {{fees_mar_apr_ll}} in DefiLlama/Allium), so it is not an
  indexing artifact. Its cause cannot be identified from aggregate data.
- **Buybacks are held, not burned.** The bought-back NEAR sits as native balances in a multisig and a DAO account. NEAR's own tokenomics post says
  governance "would retain control of the revenue, with the ability to redeploy assets" and that "no specific mechanism is guaranteed".
  Total supply keeps growing at {{rate_after}} a year regardless.
- **No usage series reliably leads price.** {{ll_sig}} of {{ll_n}} weekly lead-lag tests pass a Holm-adjusted 5% threshold
  ({{ll_raw}} before correction). The history is short ({{ll_weeks}} weeks), so this is weak evidence either way.

## What would change our mind

- **Toward bullish:** usage covering at least 50% of issuance over two consecutive 90-day windows; net capture above
  {{be_bps}} bps while Intents volume keeps growing year over year; an onchain burn or lock of the NEAR held by
  `buybacks.multisignature.near`; or a lead-lag test from usage to price that survives correction after at least another year of data.
- **Toward bearish:** 90-day coverage below 10% (it was {{d90_cov}}); year-over-year Intents volume growth turning negative
  (it was {{iv_yoy}}); the buyback wallet balance falling (largest drop between samples so far: {{bb_drop}}); or governance
  redirecting captured revenue away from buybacks.
- **On the method:** if an independent source (BigQuery, the Intents Explorer API) disagrees with DefiLlama's Intents revenue
  by more than the gap we see against revenue.near.org ({{off_max}} pp in {{off_max_m}}), the coverage numbers here are wrong.

## What we could not compute

- **NEAR Intents Explorer API.** It needs a partner JWT. We use DefiLlama's open-source `near-intents` adapter instead, which
  states that it reconciles with revenue.near.org; chart 4 checks that claim.
- **BigQuery (`bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us`).** Queries need a billed Google Cloud project. We use the NearBlocks
  indexer and check it against archival RPC block headers and against DefiLlama/Allium.
- **Staking and locking.** NEAR's "effective emissions" definition also subtracts staked or locked NEAR; our effective emissions
  subtract only burn and buybacks.
- **Revenue by channel** (front-end, partners, quote improvement) is shown on revenue.near.org but not in a public time series.
- **NEAR AI / Agent Market revenue** has no public time series.
- **The cause of the April 2026 L1 activity drop** cannot be identified from aggregate counts.
